Abstract

Photorealistic Style Transfer (PST) aims to transfer the color and tonal style of a reference to a content image while strictly preserving its structural integrity. However, existing deep learning-based methods inherently suffer from semantic entanglement caused by pre-trained image encoders, leading to unnatural spatial distortions. Moreover, current pixel-level mapping paradigms often ignore color gamut topology, resulting in color banding, while also lacking the multimodal capability for precise, tone-specific text-driven control.

To address these bottlenecks, we propose StatLUT, an innovative multimodal framework for 3D LUT generation. First, we bypass traditional encoders and introduce a Lab-Extractor to derive spatially-agnostic statistical features, fundamentally decoupling color distributions from structural semantics to ensure artifact-free rendering. Second, we formulate LUT generation as a Transformer-based Seq2Seq translation task, utilizing a Multi-dimensional Residual Mapper (MR-Mapper) to predict topologically smooth 3D LUTs. Finally, to break the unimodal barrier, we propose the H-Diffuser, a lightweight Diffusion Transformer that directly synthesizes statistical features from tone-descriptive text prompts, enabling controllable and robust color grading. Extensive experiments on standard benchmarks demonstrate that StatLUT significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both visual quality and quantitative metrics, pioneering a highly robust and versatile paradigm for multimodal photorealistic style transfer.

Method

Overview of the StatLUT framework. The framework consists of three key components: (1) Lab-Extractor for spatially-agnostic statistical feature extraction, (2) MR-Mapper for Transformer-based 3D LUT prediction, and (3) H-Diffuser for text-driven statistical feature synthesis.

1 Lab-Extractor

Bypasses traditional encoders and derives spatially-agnostic statistical features from the Lab color space, fundamentally decoupling color distributions from structural semantics to ensure artifact-free rendering.

2 MR-Mapper

Formulates LUT generation as a Transformer-based Seq2Seq translation task, utilizing a Multi-dimensional Residual Mapper to predict topologically smooth 3D LUTs with proper color gamut topology preservation.

3 H-Diffuser

A lightweight Diffusion Transformer that directly synthesizes statistical features from tone-descriptive text prompts, enabling controllable and robust color grading beyond the unimodal barrier.

Training & Inference

The training pipeline optimizes the MR-Mapper for accurate 3D LUT prediction while ensuring topological smoothness. During inference, StatLUT supports both image-reference and text-driven color grading modes.
